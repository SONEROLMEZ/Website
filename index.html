About me

I document the feeling of a place, not just the view.

My work lives between travel, photography, and memory. I’m drawn to places with texture, narrative, and a sense of rhythm — where people, architecture, and atmosphere all feel connected.

Whether I’m photographing a wedding, creating editorial imagery, or exploring a new city, I aim to tell stories that feel honest, intimate, and alive.
